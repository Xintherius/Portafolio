# Global Video Game Sales & Market Trends Analysis

An exploratory data analysis and business intelligence project analyzing historical global video game sales across platforms, genres, publishers, and geographical regions.

---

## Project Overview
This project investigates the historical commercial performance of over 16,000 video game titles to identify:
- Regional market divergence (North America, Europe, Japan, and Global).
- Genre popularity and revenue distribution across different console generations.
- Market share and commercial longevity of leading publishers.
